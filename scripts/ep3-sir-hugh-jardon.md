## 4. The arms — 1:40 to 2:30

*Asset 03 Sir Hugh's shield, then asset 04 heraldry lower third.*

I helped Josh design his coat of arms, demanding it be accurate to historical heraldry rules. A purple field with a golden tower, and two golden roundels at its base. In heraldry speak, that's purpure, a tower between two bezants Or.

A bezant, for the record, is a gold roundel, named after an old Byzantine gold coin. That is a genuine fact, and it's the only part of this design I'm explaining.

It even follows the rule of tincture from last week: gold is a metal, purple's a colour, so it reads from across the table. Whether you'd want it to is up to you

## 5. Prep and metals — 2:30 to 3:00

*Clips 3.1 to 3.3: speed-up of the airbrush, then steel. Asset 05 paint swatches from here. Asset 06 Gaahleri lower third.*

Same start as the chequy knight: grey primer through the Gaahleri TurboX Mini. They sent me that for free, and the affiliate link's in the description. Then Vallejo Air Steel with a Black Templar wash for the armour.

## 6. The shield — 3:00 to 5:30

*Clip 3.4: overhead, locked off. This is the Short.*

Hive Fleet Purple 1 for the field first. But gold over purple just disappears, so the trick is to undercoat the shapes in Blackstone Void: the tower, and both… bezants. [Reaction.]

Then Hawkshroud Yellow on top. Then my favourite trick from the chequy shield: a fine dark line round every edge. It hides every wobble.

Then bricks. Little staggered lines on the tower. It is a very well-built tower. Structurally, it's extremely sound. A black door, a white shine on each bezant, and a Shading Medium glaze at the bottom so the whole shield looks curved.

[How you feel about the finished shield. Keep it heraldic.]

## 7. Caparison, surcoat and lance — 5:30 to 6:30

*Clips 3.5, 3.6, 3.12 and 3.13 for the caparison and its bezants. Clips 3.14 and 3.15 for the rider and lance.*

The caparison is a big area, so the purple went on with the airbrush, then Hive Fleet Purple 2 and 3 to bring up the folds. And because one pair of bezants clearly wasn't enough, he's got a gold bezant on each flank as well, with a brighter yellow to make them shine. The rider's surcoat is purple with gold trim, and the lance and pennant are gold and purple.

## 8. The black horse — 6:30 to 7:30

*Clips 3.7 to 3.11: horse on its own, then next to the rider.*

For the horse I went black. The chequy knight is on a grey horse, so this keeps the unit varied, and black lets the purple and gold do all the talking. Blackstone Void 1 for the coat, highlighted up with Dataslate Grey, then a touch of pale blue and Necrolith Bone on the very highest points, so he looks glossy rather than flat. Saddle and straps in Carthalos Skin, steel barding, and a Black Templar line round everything to tidy it up.
